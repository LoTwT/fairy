- `anomaly_damage`

- `disorder_daze`

- Part 06 part break formula areas
- Part 07 interrupt formula areas
- complete character, Drive Disc, W-Engine, Bangboo, enemy, skill, anomaly, equipment,
  or runtime fixture databases

These areas require later source review, observed effect evidence, detailed fixture inputs,
and separate PRs before Phase 6 can implement them.
